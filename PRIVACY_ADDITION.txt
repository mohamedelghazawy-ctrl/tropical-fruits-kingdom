English:
Usage and crash data: This game uses Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) to improve gameplay. We collect anonymous data such as levels played and outcomes, crash reports (device model, Android version, crash cause) and an app-installation identifier. We do not collect your name, email or location. Data is processed by Google under its Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
